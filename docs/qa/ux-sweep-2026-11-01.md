# UX sweep of the main portal, 2026-11-01

Owner request ("refine UI/UX and standard"). Standard: `docs/coord/UI-STANDARD.md`, `docs/prd/ui-layout.md`, QA-STANDARDS A1-A10.
Out of scope (other branches): WindowedList / full-page list windowing, the Admin console, the reporter panel (`feat/ui-standard-2`), and
performance / lazy loading (`perf/local-wins`).

## How

- Base `origin/release/ui-standard` (70dd4b0), branch `ux/sweep`. Local only (the k3s cluster was down).
- A production-mode scratch portal (`SCRATCH_PRODUCTION=1 scripts/scratch-portal.sh start 3637`), seeded with `tools/qa/seed-scale.mjs`
  (500 sessions, crews, 25k / 10k-event transcripts, 6,000 usage rows) and over the API: two orgs, three teams, three invitations (one
  long address), a second bucket, 36 tickets in every status with comments, two vaults; 40 Inbox rows written to `notify_history`.
  Workspaces and Claudes stay empty (no Kubernetes or Docker in scratch), so those pages were seen in their empty state.
- Every page and every tab (78 views) in headless Chromium at 390 (touch, safe-area insets 47 / 34), 1280 x 800 and 1584 x 900, light
  and dark, with a screenshot and measures per view: sideways scroll, boxes past the right edge, controls under 44 px (phone), spinners,
  alerts, and whether the document scrolls on a desktop (it must not: only `#main-scroll`). Plus sign-in, the org switcher, the dock.
- Screenshots: `/tmp/claude-1000/-workspaces-claudepilot/6b18418b-4e83-41b3-ab37-55ab385890a8/scratchpad/ux-sweep/`
  (`before-<page>[-<tab>]-<size>-<theme>.png`, `after-...` for 390 and 1280 after the fixes; `qa/<script>/` for the QA runs).
  Measures: `before-measures.json`, `after-measures.json` beside them. (Scratch paths: not kept after the session.)

### Seen, not changed (with the reason)

| Page | Observation | Why not here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Needs you vs Home "Needs you" tile | The tile lists approvals, interrupted chats, CI and tickets (47); the page showed "Nothing needs you" on the seeded data. Two things share one name. | Different sources by design (`glance/widgets/attention/needs.ts` vs the attention API); the seeded approvals are not live. Naming is a product call: raise on a ticket. |
| Home > Tickets tile | Five stats in a 22 rem tile: the fifth ("In review") wraps to a row of its own. | Needs a decision on which stat to drop or a smaller stat; not a one-liner. |
| Dock | Two widgets both titled "Agents" (sessions, running agents). | Widget naming is the glance PRD's; raise on a ticket. |
| Org settings tabs | Each tab repeats its name as a section title ("General" under the General tab). | Several sections hang their InfoTip and actions on that title; removing it is a design change across 10 sections. |
| Org settings > Sign-in | Checkboxes are 20 px. | False positive: each sits in a 44 px label row, which is the target. |
| Agents (desktop) | A glance chip row under the title repeats the side bar's chips. | It is the person's own layout choice (`places.header`). |
| Sign-in (desktop) | The logo sits at the left of a 64 rem bar, the form in a 25 rem column. | The decided pay frame. |
| Schedules | A segmented All / Loops / Triggers / Helpers above the list. | A filter of one list, not a section picker. |
