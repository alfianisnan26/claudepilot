## 2. Principles
D1. THE POLICY IS DATA, EDITABLE IN SETTINGS: tiers (strongest, strong, standard, light) mapped to concrete model ids and efforts (using the model families and versions the portal already knows), a rubric text (the five criteria) that is delivered to a parent agent as instruction text, allowed models and efforts per tier, a default tier, a cap on the highest tier a child may get, and the escalation rules. Defaults are conservative.
D2. ENFORCED ON THE SERVER, NEVER TRUSTED FROM THE PARENT: the spawn tool takes optional `model`, `effort` and a short `reason`; the portal CLAMPS to the policy (an unknown or forbidden choice is refused or lowered, with the reason recorded and told to the parent), requires a reason when the choice is above the default tier, and stores model, effort, reason, the tier, who chose (the parent session or a person) and whether it was clamped on the child session. The reason is untrusted text: clipped, sanitised, shown as plain text.
D3. ESCALATION IS OPT-IN AND BOUNDED: off by default. Criteria the portal can observe or the parent can report (a child reported stuck; repeated failed tool or test results; an explicit request), one tier at a time, a maximum number of escalations per tree and per child, a BUDGET cap per delegation tree (estimated cost, labelled as an estimate, from the usage engine), and the strongest tier only when the policy allows it. An escalation continues from the child's status and transcript summary (a NEW child session started by the portal like a spawn, linked to the stuck one), never silently: a visible card, an audit entry, a notification once the event bus exists, and a stop button.
D4. QUOTA-AWARE, HONESTLY: when the usage engine says a model's Claude is near its limit, the policy may prefer a lighter tier or another Claude; numbers shown are estimates with their basis; unavailable data is said to be unavailable.
D5. EVERYTHING VISIBLE AND AUDITED: the agent tree (AgentsPanel) and the Active agents widget show model, effort and the reason (the reason in an InfoTip), clamps and escalations are marked; every change is audited with actor, before and after, and why: policy edits, each spawn's choice, each escalation, each person's override.
D6. THE PERSON CAN ALWAYS OVERRIDE: change a running child's model or effort (the existing controls), stop it, or turn escalation off at once; an override is audited.
D7. EXTEND, DO NOT REBUILD; NO COLLISIONS (U4 owns the widget until it reports done: you add chips through a surgical edit then); text diet; abuse tests: a parent asking for a model outside the policy, a reason carrying instructions or markup, a spawn storm, an escalation loop, budget exhaustion mid-tree, policy edits by a non-admin (once multi-user lands).
