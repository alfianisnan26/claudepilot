# S2: real-device checklist items for the btw (iOS Safari, Android Chrome)
- iOS Safari: type "/" in the composer: the command list shows above the keyboard bar and a tap on "/btw" completes it; the btw icon button asks what is written (or inserts "/btw " when the box is empty).
- iOS Safari: the btw sheet opens half height above the composer with the keyboard closed; focusing the composer (keyboard up) keeps the composer visible above the keyboard and the sheet drops to half; the sheet never covers the composer or goes under the header.
- iOS Safari: drag the sheet handle up (full height) and down (back to half, then closes past ~90 px); the page behind does not scroll or rubber-band while dragging; Esc is n/a, the Close button works.
- iOS Safari, installed app (PWA): same with the home-indicator safe area; the sheet bottom sits on the composer, not under the indicator.
- Phone landscape: the card (not a sheet) leaves the transcript usable; the composer and keyboard do not overlap.
- A main turn streaming while a btw is open: the transcript keeps its place (unpinned) or stays at the end (pinned) on a real touch scroll.
- VoiceOver / TalkBack: focus lands on the card when a btw is asked, the answer is announced once when it ends (polite), Close returns focus to the message box.
