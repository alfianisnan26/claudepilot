### How it was checked

- **Scanner and validators, the repository's own code:** a tsx script (kept in the session scratchpad, not in the repo) ran `scanSkill` and `parseSkillMd` over each of the nine skill folders, `PersonaKind.parse` over `pilot-way.json` and `ContextKind.parse` over every context file. It ran before and after the fixes.
- **Hidden characters:** a scan of every in-scope file for non-ASCII. The only one is `§` in `persona/README.md`. No bidi, zero-width or tag characters.
- **Tool names:** every backticked `snake_case` name was checked against the `name: '…'` registrations. Every name that does not exist (`session_context`, `decision_*`, `peer_review_*`, `playbook_search`, `scoreboard_me`, `my_efficiency`, `project_create`) is marked "(when available)", or "planned" in the README. The other names exist. Ticket statuses match `TICKET_STATUSES`, and the types `incident`, `question` and `approval` exist. Assignee values `none`, `user` and `session:<id>` match `assigneeOf`. "Only a person reopens a closed ticket" matches the `ticket_close` description.
- **Limits, after the fixes:** every SKILL.md is between 5.4 and 9.1 KB (limit 16 KB). Frontmatter has `name` and `description` only, and the name equals the folder. Descriptions run 306 to 530 characters and all start "Use when". The skills have at most 2 reference files each, the largest 5.4 KB. Contexts are 2.1 to 4.8 KB (limit 20 KB). The persona parses: instructions 10.9 KB and 106 lines (warning at 200), 9 agents, every description 300 characters or less, chatPrompt 1.3 KB. Final scanner result: risk **low** for all nine skills, with no findings, warnings or refusals.
