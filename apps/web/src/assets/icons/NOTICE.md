# Icons

Model and provider glyphs for the Agents tree (T-1825), copied unchanged from
[homarr-labs/dashboard-icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons), licensed under the
[Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons/blob/main/LICENSE). Downloaded 2026-10-20 and served from the
bundle (no runtime fetch). The marks themselves belong to their owners and are used only to name the model a card runs on.

`openai.svg` and `ollama.svg` are black; the app inverts them in dark mode (`dark:invert`). The set's `openai-light.svg` was not
used: its fill is not a valid colour.
